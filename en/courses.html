English and admissions exam courses

The three courses build everyday English, go deeper into IB English Literature, and prepare for the SAT, TOEFL and IELTS. The leaflet does not list a fee for each course. Please enquire for the detailed arrangements.

1

Foundation English training

Improves overall listening, speaking, reading and writing, builds a language foundation, and gives students more confidence to speak English.

2

IB English Literature

Goes deeper into literary appreciation and critical thinking, and improves academic writing and analysis for the demands of the IB.

3

SAT, TOEFL and IELTS admissions exams

Professional preparation courses focused on exam technique and strategy, helping students reach their target scores and apply to the schools they hope to attend.

How these differ from international school assessments

Kindergarten Phonics, and primary MAP Test, CAT 4 and IDAT, belong to Hong Kong international school admissions training. See admissions service A. The three courses on this page train English ability and university admissions exams.
