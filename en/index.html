Four core arrangements

Services and courses follow the items listed on the Lead Education leaflet. Items A and B show fee ranges. For boarding high school planning and English course fees, please ask an adviser directly.

A

Hong Kong international schools

Kindergarten behaviour learning and Phonics, plus primary preparation for MAP Test, CAT 4, IDAT and other assessments.

B

University applications

Strategy, school selection and the admissions process for universities in the UK, the US and Hong Kong, covering UCAS and the Common App.

C

Boarding high school

Plans US boarding school applications for junior secondary students, and helps them choose between UK and US high schools.

D

Course overview

Foundation English, IB English Literature, and SAT, TOEFL and IELTS admissions exam preparation.

Who these arrangements are for

Each group matches the stage and application goal covered by the corresponding service.

Families preparing for Hong Kong international schools

Need kindergarten behaviour learning, a Phonics foundation, or primary MAP Test, CAT 4 and IDAT preparation, plus interview coaching.

Junior secondary students planning boarding high school

Need application essays and interview training for US boarding schools, and school positioning between UK and US high schools.

Students applying to universities in the UK, the US or Hong Kong

Need a school list, a strategy for applying to more than one country at the same time, and follow-through from application to enrolment and scholarship procedures.

Students who need to strengthen English or exam skills

Want to improve listening, speaking, reading and writing, study IB English Literature, or prepare for the SAT, TOEFL or IELTS.
