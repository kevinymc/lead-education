About us

Lead Education 立揚教育

Lead Education arranges Hong Kong international school admissions training, UK and US boarding high school applications, and school selection and application follow-up for universities in the UK, the US and Hong Kong. Courses are designed around gaps in English foundation, helping students prepare for assessments, interviews and academic writing.

How advisers help with school selection

For boarding high school, the advisory team knows schools across the United States. Based on each high school’s administration and character, they choose suitable boarding schools for junior secondary students and guide students as they choose between UK and US high schools. University applications cover strategic planning, school selection and the admissions process.

How training supports applications

The international school programme covers kindergarten behaviour learning and Phonics, and primary MAP Test, CAT 4 and IDAT. There are also foundation English, IB English Literature, and SAT, TOEFL and IELTS preparation courses, supporting listening, speaking, reading, writing and exam skills.
