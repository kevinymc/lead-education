Admissions services

Interviews, school selection and the application process

The three admissions services cover Hong Kong international schools; universities in the UK, the US and Hong Kong; and UK and US boarding high schools. The fees below are the ranges published on the leaflet.

A

Hong Kong international school admissions

Interview, school selection and assessment training. Lead Education’s dedicated courses are designed around gaps in English foundation, helping children meet the standard.

The content covers kindergarten behaviour learning and a Phonics foundation, plus primary preparation for MAP Test, CAT 4, IDAT and other assessments. Students receive one-to-one interview coaching, English expression training and mock tests, building confidence, language ability and exam technique.

HK$18,000 – HK$38,000+

One-to-one interview coaching English expression training Mock test assessment Building confidence and exam technique

Assessments this service covers

Kindergarten focuses on behaviour learning and Phonics. Primary covers MAP Test, CAT 4 and IDAT, together with interviews and mock tests.

B

University application strategy and school selection

UCAS / Common App. LE provides application services for universities in the UK, the US and Hong Kong, covering strategic planning, school selection and the admissions process.

HK$35,000 – HK$65,000

Targeted school selection

Recommends 5–10 universities that match expectations, for example the G5, the Ivy League, and UK and US Top 30, grouped into reach, ambitious and suitable.

Multi-country strategy

For applications to the UK, the US and Hong Kong at the same time, arranges the timing of US undergraduate early applications and UK undergraduate applications such as Oxford and Cambridge, seeking a higher chance of admission.

End-to-end handling

From the start of the application through to enrolment, including scholarship applications and offer procedures.

C

UK and US boarding high school applications and planning

Lead Education provides US boarding school admissions applications and planning for junior secondary students, including application essay guidance and interview training.

The advisory team knows the administration and character of high schools across the United States, selects suitable boarding schools for students, and guides students as they choose between UK and US high schools.

Application planning Essay guidance Interview training School positioning End-to-end follow-up

This item does not list a standard fee on the leaflet. You are welcome to enquire by WhatsApp, WeChat or email.
